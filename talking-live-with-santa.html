Questions parents ask

Good to know

Is it a real person playing Santa?

No. Santa is an AI-powered video avatar modeled on a real Santa Claus. He listens and answers in about a second, so it feels like a real conversation, and most children never notice the difference. There's no actor on the other end, and we ask every parent to confirm they understand this before booking.

Will Santa see my child?

No. Your camera is never turned on. Santa only hears your child through the microphone.

What happens to my child's information?

It's used only to personalize the call. We never sell or share it with anyone. If you don't buy the keepsake video, the conversation isn't kept.

My child doesn't speak, or uses a communication device. Will this work?

Yes. Tell us on the booking form and Santa will slow down, ask simple yes-or-no questions, and wait patiently for answers. A parent can also speak for the child or help them answer.

What age is this for?

Santa talks with children of all ages and abilities. A parent or guardian should be in the room.

What do we need?

A computer, tablet, or phone with a microphone and an internet connection. It works from a bed, a wheelchair, or a hospital room. Headphones are optional.
